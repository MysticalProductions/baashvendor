Privacy Policy

This registration portal collects information needed to onboard and verify BAASH vendors, including business details, owner contact information and KYC documents.

1. Information collected

Information may include venue and legal business details, owner name, email, mobile number, GST/PAN information, address, location information selected through the form, uploaded KYC documents and the electronic signature.

2. Purpose

Information is used for vendor onboarding, identity/business verification, compliance checks, communication, record keeping and creation of the vendor agreement.

3. Storage

Registration data and submitted documents are stored in systems used by BAASH for onboarding operations. Access should be restricted to authorised personnel and service accounts.

4. Third-party services

The portal uses Google Apps Script/Google Drive/Google Sheets for processing and storage and a public location-data API for state/city selection. The location API receives the selected country/state query, not the vendor's KYC documents.

5. Contact

For registration or privacy questions, contact BAASH at 979496764.
